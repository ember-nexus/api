 Error 422 - Unprocessable Content

 The request content was syntactically valid, but could not be processed due to a configured resource limit, e.g. an
 expression (Go Expr Lang) query exceeding its configured timeout.

 Link to the documentation: https://ember-nexus.github.io/api
