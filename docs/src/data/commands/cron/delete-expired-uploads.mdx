Permanently deletes resumable uploads (started via [`POST /<uuid>/file`](/api/post-element-file) or
[`PUT /<uuid>/file`](/api/put-element-file)) that expired and were never completed, freeing their
already-uploaded chunks from storage.

Takes no arguments or options. An upload becomes eligible once its `expires` timestamp (see
[`PATCH /upload/<uuid>`](/api/patch-upload)) is further in the past than the configured grace period - the
grace period is added on top of the expiry itself, so an upload isn't deleted the instant it
expires. Uploads that were explicitly deleted with [`DELETE /upload/<uuid>`](/api/delete-upload),
or removed automatically because their target element was deleted, are skipped since there is
nothing left to clean up.

<Note>
    Normally run as part of [`cron`](/command/cron), not invoked directly. It's safe to run at any
    time, though - it only ever touches uploads that are already expired.
</Note>
