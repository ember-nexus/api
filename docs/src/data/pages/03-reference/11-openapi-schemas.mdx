title: OpenAPI schemas
---

OpenAPI schemas are the reusable data structures defined under `components/schemas` in
the OpenAPI specification. They describe the shape of request bodies, response
payloads, and individual field types used throughout the API.

Most entries here are **fragments** — small, focused objects that are composed
together rather than returned directly by an endpoint. They document the exact
fields, types, and constraints you can expect when interacting with the API.
